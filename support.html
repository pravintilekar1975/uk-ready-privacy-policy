Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. UK Ready's learning features do not require you to create an account.

Where is my study progress saved?

Study progress and preferences are saved locally on your device. Depending on your device settings, some data may also be included in operating-system backups. See our Privacy Policy for more information.

How do I reset my learning progress?

Open Settings in UK Ready and select Reset Learning Progress. Review the confirmation message before continuing. This removes saved learning progress and cannot be undone within the app. Your language and text-size preferences are retained.

Can I study in English and Urdu?

Yes. UK Ready supports English and Urdu. Use the app's language selection controls to choose your preferred language.

What should I do if the app is not working as expected?

Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue continues, email mamax.support@gmail.com with a brief description so we can investigate.
