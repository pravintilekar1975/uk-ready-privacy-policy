UK Ready — Privacy Policy

How UK Ready handles information and protects your privacy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

UK Ready is a Life in the UK Test preparation application developed under the MAMAX Technologies brand.

At MAMAX Technologies, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how UK Ready handles information, stores learning progress and protects your data.

1. Information We Collect

UK Ready does not require users to create an account, register or provide personal information to access its learning features.

The current version of UK Ready is designed to work without collecting personal information through advertising networks, external analytics services or developer-operated servers.

If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and provide support.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

UK Ready stores information locally on your device to support your learning experience. This includes:

  • Study Book progress, bookmarks and reading positions
  • Practice question results and progress
  • Mock examination results and history
  • Mistake tracking and learning progress
  • English or Urdu language preferences
  • Study Book text-size preferences

This information is stored using local application storage and is not transmitted to MAMAX Technologies through the app's learning features.

3. Advertising and Analytics

UK Ready does not currently integrate third-party advertising networks or external analytics services.

The app may calculate scores, identify areas for improvement and display learning statistics. These calculations are intended to help users monitor their progress and do not require sending learning records to MAMAX Technologies.

4. Device Backups

Depending on your device, operating system and backup settings, locally stored UK Ready information may be included in system backups, such as Android device backups or Apple device backups.

These backups are managed through your device and associated platform services, not through servers operated by MAMAX Technologies.

Backed-up information may be restored when you reinstall the app or change devices. You can manage your backup preferences through your device or platform account settings.

5. Deleting Your Learning Data

UK Ready provides a Reset Learning Progress feature.

This removes locally stored learning records, including practice progress, mock examination history, mistake tracking, Study Book progress, bookmarks and reading positions.

Your language and text-size preferences are retained so that you can continue using the app with your preferred settings.

You may also remove locally stored application data by uninstalling the app or clearing its data where supported by your device.

Resetting or uninstalling the app may not automatically remove information previously included in operating-system backups.

6. Sharing of Information

MAMAX Technologies does not sell users' learning progress or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

If you contact us for support, your email is processed through our email service provider to enable us to receive and respond to your message.

Apple and Google may independently process information when you download or use applications through their platforms. Their processing is governed by their respective privacy policies.

7. Data Retention

Learning progress and preferences remain in local application storage until they are deleted, reset or otherwise removed by the device or operating system.

If you contact us by email, we retain correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, manage support and meet any applicable legal obligations.

8. Data Security

UK Ready is designed to minimise unnecessary collection and transmission of user information.

Learning records are stored using the device's application storage facilities. Their protection also depends on your device's security settings and operating system.

No electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

UK Ready is designed primarily for people preparing for the Life in the UK Test and is not specifically directed at children.

The application does not require users to register an account or submit personal information to access its educational features.

10. Your Privacy Rights

You can reset locally stored learning progress using the app's Reset Learning Progress feature.

If you have contacted us by email, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information held in connection with your enquiry, subject to applicable legal requirements.

For privacy-related requests, contact us using the email address below.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to UK Ready, its features or applicable privacy requirements.

Any updated version will be published with a revised effective date.
